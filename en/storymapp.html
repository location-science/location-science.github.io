StoryMapp. Stories, where they happened.

Yes, with two p's. A free app for iOS and Android that shows you the stories around you: more than 7.5 million places in six languages, as text and as audio.

  1. 1.0

    Open the map.

    Wherever you stand, the map shows the places around you that have a story: history, culture and the people who lived there. That holds in big cities as much as in small villages. Thanks to the global community the stories change and grow every minute, and our pipeline keeps them current.

    Your location stays on your phone and is never sent to our servers. We store favourites and history only if you sign in.

  2. 2.0

    Listen in your language.

    Every story comes as text and as audio, in German, English, French, Spanish, Portuguese and Chinese.

    We research the stories with our own trained AI models and adapt them to what the reader needs. The models stay close to the sources, because we would rather say less about a place than say something wrong.

  3. 3.0

    Share your own tours.

    Every organisation has stories that belong to places: a collective's mural, a village's trail, a zoo's animals, a museum's collection. StoryMapp is open to you for that, and your tours sit on the same map as millions of other places.

    • Curated tours and routes. Stops in your order, with a walking route on the map.
    • Your stories, your voice. Text, audio and images with credits and licences, managed in a web dashboard.
    • On the map and in search. Visitors nearby find your project, and through it your website.
    • Your brand in the app. Your logo and colours accompany your tours.
    • Every language. Built-in translation, with you in control of every version.
    • Links and QR codes. Every place and tour has a permanent link. A printed code on site is enough, with or without the app.
    • Vouchers and local partners. Show businesses from your area, with vouchers and advertising inside the stories.
    • For everyone who tells stories. Museums, city marketing, tourism boards, zoos, botanical gardens, the arts, heritage associations.

    We set you up personally and accompany your first tour.

  4. 4.0

    Put the stories in your product.

    StoryMapp's content is also available through an API, for tourism platforms, travel providers, navigation systems and other services. That is how you add stories about places to your booking site, your hotel app or your map.

    Ask about the API

StoryMapp is made by Location Science GmbH, a software and data company from Upper Bavaria that has contributed to open map data for years. The app respects its users: no location tracking and no content we cannot stand behind.
