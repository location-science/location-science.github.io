Software, data and AI that work every day.

Location Science is a software and data company from Upper Bavaria. We digitalise processes, introduce AI and make geodata usable, for public authorities and for companies. With StoryMapp we have a product of our own that shows how we work.

Does this sound familiar?

  • Data sits in many systems, does not fit together and has to be normalised first.
  • Management wants AI. Nobody knows where it adds value and what is already possible today.
  • Geodata exists, but nobody brings it into an app. Important projects stay on the shelf.
  • Processes cost time because data is entered and checked by hand.
  • The tools you bought do not fit your own problems.

That is exactly where we start: with interfaces, automation and software that fits your task.

Focus areas

  • Digitalise processes

    Paper, email and spreadsheets become an application connected to your existing systems. Interfaces and automation replace manual work and error checking.

  • Introduce AI

    We check where AI already adds value in your organisation, build it in there and measure the result. Where it does not fit, we say so.

  • Make geodata usable

    Data becomes maps, apps and services. Spatial databases, normalisation, OGC interfaces, offline on the device or public in the app.

  • Operations. We operate what we build: deployment, monitoring, DevOps.
  • Open data. We build on open data and contribute to OpenStreetMap.
  • Privacy by design. GDPR from day one, developed and operated in Germany.
  • Three founders. No intermediaries. You talk to the people who write the code.

Open data shows the ways. Context decides which one is right.
