Software, data and AI that work every day.

Location Science is a software and data company from Upper Bavaria. We digitalise processes, build applications and introduce AI where it brings a real benefit. Our speciality is software, artificial intelligence and geodata. We work for public authorities and companies, and with StoryMapp we have a product of our own that shows how we work.

Competencies

  • Digitalisation and application development

    We build web and mobile applications and accompany them from the first conversation into operation.

    Paper, email and spreadsheets become software and services that carry your processes reliably.

  • Introducing AI

    We bring AI into your organisation, with a clear benefit, checked sources and firm rules for data protection.

    First we check whether AI is the right solution for your task at all. If yes, we build it in and measure the result. If no, we tell you so plainly.

  • Software engineering

    Clean code, tests and documentation are part of the work, so the software can still be maintained in five years.

  • Databases and data pipelines

    We build databases and automated data flows that run without manual work. That is how the pipeline behind StoryMapp keeps more than 7.5 million places current.

    {% include pipeline-diagram.html %}

  • Data science

    Analysis and machine learning on your data: we find patterns, assess data quality and measure what a change achieves.

  • Geodata and spatial intelligence

    Places are our strength. We turn coordinates into context and answer the questions that matter: what is here, what is nearby, and what is important to the person on site right now?

  • Mobile engineering

    Native apps for iOS and Android that also work without a network. Location stays on the device.

  • Navigation

    Positioning, routing and guidance, from car navigation, over trail hiking, to city walking tours. The computation runs on the device, not on a server.

  • StoryMapp, our own product

    Free on iOS and Android, with more than 7.5 million places and their stories in six languages. About StoryMapp →

  • Consulting

    What to build, what to buy, how to run it? We advise on digitalisation, software engineering, AI adoption and spatial data management, honestly and without slide decks.
