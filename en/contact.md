# Contact

Write to us, call us or come by. One of the three founders always answers, not a sales team and not a ticket system.

## Email

[contact@location-science.com](mailto:contact@location-science.com)

A keyword in the subject line helps us route your message, for example "Public sector enquiry", "Company enquiry", "Consulting" or "StoryMapp partner organisation". We usually reply within two working days.
