What we bring to your project

We take on defined work packages or reinforce your team for a phase. Either way you talk directly to the people who write the code.

  • Software engineering

    Backends, APIs and web front-ends, with code review and tests as a matter of course. Software that lasts.

  • Introduce AI

    We find the use case with real benefit, build the solution and measure the result. We use language models, vision models or classic machine learning, whichever the task needs.

  • Digitalise processes

    Manual workflows become software connected to your systems, from concept to operation.

  • Geodata and spatial intelligence

    Spatial databases, data pipelines, matching and analysis: coordinates become context your product can use.

  • Mobile and navigation

    Native apps for iOS and Android with offline operation, and positioning and routing directly on the device.

  • Advise and operate

    What to build, what to buy, how to run it? We advise honestly, and we operate what we build.

Engagement models

We adapt to your procurement, not the other way round.

Time and material
One daily rate for development and consulting: €850 net, invoiced monthly against timesheets.
Fixed-price works contract
For clearly specifiable work packages with acceptance. We work out the specification and milestones together with you.
Framework agreement
Call-off quotas for ongoing development, maintenance or consulting.
Subcontracting
We work under your contract with the end customer, without external attribution if you prefer. Confidentiality under NDA goes without saying.
Location
Remote is the rule, and we come on site when it helps the project. We are based between Munich, Ingolstadt and Augsburg.
Capacity
Three founders and no third-party placement. We say early what is realistic in what time.

Who this is for

Companies that digitalise processes, adopt AI or work with places.

  • Mid-sized companies that want to digitalise processes and introduce AI.
  • Software houses and GIS integrators that need geodata or mobile expertise.
  • Navigation and automotive, from map data to on-device positioning.
  • Logistics and mobility, where routing, location and data pipelines carry operations.
  • Tourism and destinations that offer location-based content and apps.
  • Utilities and infrastructure, with spatial data about networks and assets.

The founders bring working experience from start-ups and large companies, from global car navigation, defence engineering, public safety and DevOps. References by sector on request.

How a project runs

1. First conversation
Free of charge, remote or at your office. We listen, and afterwards we tell you honestly whether we are the right fit.
2. Offer
Scope, model, effort and schedule. For subcontracts we align with your end-customer contract.
3. Delivery
In your tools and processes, in short steps with fixed contacts. The source code is yours.
4. Handover
Documentation and training for your team, and if you wish, operation and maintenance.
