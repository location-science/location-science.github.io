Digitalisation and AI for the public sector.

We digitalise administrative processes, build specialist applications and mobile apps, and introduce AI where it helps. Geodata is our speciality. We work for public authorities, municipalities and public-safety organisations, as contractor, subcontractor or consultant, remote and on site.

What we do for you

Six fields in which we develop and operate ourselves. You work directly with the three founders, not with placed third parties.

  • Digitalise administrative processes

    Paper and spreadsheets become an application that carries the process from application to file and connects to your existing systems.

  • Introduce AI

    We check where AI adds value in your administration and build it in there. We comply with the GDPR and the EU AI Act along the way. Where AI does not fit, we tell you.

  • Build specialist applications

    Web applications for case handling and citizen services, built for many years of operation and further development.

  • Mobile apps for field and emergency services

    Native apps for iOS and Android that also work without a network. Location stays on the device.

  • Geodata and data pipelines

    Spatial databases, automated data flows and OGC-standard services that turn many sources into one reliable data basis.

  • Advise and operate

    Requirements, architecture and data strategy, plus technical help with tender documents. We run operations on your premises or in German data centres.

Commissioning us

We know how public procurement works in Germany and shape our offers accordingly.

Contract basis
We work on the German EVB-IT standard terms for services, development or maintenance, or on your own templates.
Direct award
Below the applicable value thresholds you can commission us directly. Offer and self-declarations arrive at short notice.
Framework agreement
For ongoing development, maintenance or consulting we are available for framework agreements with call-offs.
Subcontracting
For prime contractors and IT service providers we take on clearly defined work packages, above all in geodata, mobile and AI.
Pricing
Time and material with daily rates, or a fixed price under a works contract, depending on how precisely the work can be specified in advance.
Documents
Commercial register extract, self-declarations of suitability and references by sector; a data processing agreement under Art. 28 GDPR is available on request.

Sovereignty and security

This is what we can promise you, no more and no less.

  • Development in Germany, by the founders themselves.
  • Operation on your premises or in German and European data centres.
  • Open source where it serves you. Public money, public code.
  • Privacy by design and support with data protection impact assessments.
  • Accessibility under BITV 2.0 from the start.
  • Experience in security-sensitive environments: public safety and defence engineering.

We name certifications only once we hold them. Ask us which evidence your procedure needs, and we will tell you plainly what we can provide.

Experience

Three founders with backgrounds in geoinformatics, cartography and signal processing. We gained our working experience in start-ups and large companies, at public-safety organisations, in defence engineering, in global car navigation and in DevOps. References by sector on request.

One piece of evidence is public: StoryMapp, our own app with more than 7.5 million places in six languages. Its data pipeline runs without manual work, and location never leaves the device.
