StoryMapp. Stories, where they happened.

Yes, with two p’s. A free app for iOS and Android that shows you the stories around you: more than 7.5 million places and counting, in English, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese and Chinese, as text and audio.

  1. 1.0

    Open the map.

    Wherever you stand, the map shows the places around you that have a story: the history, the culture and the human experiences tied to them. From large cities to remote villages, coverage grows continuously through our automated pipeline, so what you see is current.

    Your location is processed on your phone and never sent to our servers. Favourites and history are kept only if you choose to sign in.

  2. 2.0

    Listen in your language.

    Every story is available as text and as audio, in English, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese and Chinese.

    Stories are produced with a fine-tuned model built to stay close to its sources. We work to minimise the well-known hallucination problem rather than pretend it away, which is why we would rather say less about a place than say something wrong.

  3. 3.0

    Share your own tours.

    Every organisation has stories rooted in places: the mural a collective painted, the trail a town is proud of, the animals a zoo cares for, the history a museum keeps alive. StoryMapp is open to third-party organisations, so your tours sit on the same map that already connects millions of places with the people walking past them.

    • Curated tours and routes. Ordered stops with a walking route on the map, each stop with its own story.
    • Your stories, your voice. Text, audio and images, with proper credits and licensing, managed in a web dashboard.
    • Found on the map and in search. Visitors nearby become aware of your project and follow through to your own website.
    • Your brand in the app. Your logo and colours accompany your tours.
    • Every visitor’s language. Built-in translation, with editorial control over every language kept by you.
    • Share links and QR codes. Every place and tour gets a durable link. Print a code on site and visitors jump straight into your story, app or no app.
    • Vouchers and local partners. Showcase local businesses and cooperations with vouchers and story-integrated advertisements.
    • For every storyteller. Museums, city marketing, tourism boards, zoos, botanical gardens, art collectives and heritage associations.

    We set you up personally and walk you through your first tour.

  4. 4.0

    Put the stories in your own product.

    The content behind StoryMapp is available through an API for tourism platforms, travel companies, navigation systems and other location-aware services. Add accurate, current, place-based stories to your booking site, your hotel app or your map.
