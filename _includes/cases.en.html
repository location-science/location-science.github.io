What we are working on

Four projects that show how we bring data, software and AI together. Two of them are still in development, and we say so.

  • In development

    Tour planner that builds the tour for the day

    Most tour portals show a database of ready-made tours. Our planner creates the tour for the user and the day. It combines weather, warnings and closures with fitness, time budget and preferences, and with trail surface, gradient, shade and places to rest or eat.

    The result is a route that fits, instead of a list to search through.

  • In development

    SkiApp: the ski day, measured and shared

    The app records the skier's statistics and health data and links them to the geography of the ski area. Photos and videos are embedded in a 3D replay of the run that can be shared with friends and followers.

    Behind it: a terrain model, piste data, on-device positioning, and a snow and weather report from several sources.

  • In operation

    StoryMapp: stories about places, worldwide

    Our own product, free for iOS and Android. More than 8 million stories in six languages, worldwide coverage, a monthly update cycle, users in 58 countries. Partner: the artists' project Art Hop Silver Lake in Los Angeles.

    The data pipeline behind it runs without manual work, and the users' location never leaves the device. About StoryMapp →

  • In development

    Emergency map for disaster response

    A map that works without network and without power, and is still current. It gives emergency services orientation and a basis for planning during a large-scale blackout, on the device, from current open data.
