Tobias Neumann

Managing partner

Studied geography (B.Sc.) and geoinformatics (M.Sc.) and has worked with spatial information ever since. Experience from equipping emergency control centres, developing air-defence systems and global car-navigation projects, including for TomTom. Most recently research on open geodata at a world-leading technology company. Builds and optimises spatial databases, geodata infrastructures and data compilers that turn raw data into deliverable map formats, as well as front ends for map display. A particular focus is proofs of concept that make new ideas quick to test, and agentic systems for autonomous operation.
