Location software, from pipeline to pocket.

Location Science is a software and data company in Upper Bavaria. We build location-aware applications, the data pipelines and GeoAI behind them, and ship them to Desktop, Web, iOS and Android. StoryMapp is our proof.

Competencies

  • Application development and software engineering

    Web and mobile applications, APIs and the services behind them, from requirement to release. Engineered to be maintained for years, not demonstrated once.

  • Database engineering and data pipelines

    Spatial databases, ETL and automated pipelines. The ones behind StoryMapp keep more than 7.5 million places current, unattended.

    PostGIS-class spatial storage, batch and streaming ingestion, and the monitoring to know when a source changes shape.

  • GeoAI

    Language and vision models applied to places: fine-tuned, grounded in their sources, built to minimise hallucination.

  • Spatial intelligence

    Turning coordinates into context: what is here, what is nearby, and what matters to the person standing there.

  • Data science

    Analytics and machine learning on geographic and social data: matching, extraction, ranking, evaluation.

  • Mobile engineering

    Native iOS and Android. Offline-capable, multilingual text and audio, location handled on the device.

  • Navigation

    Positioning, routing and guidance, from global car navigation to walking tours. Processed on the device, not on a server.

  • StoryMapp, our first product

    Free on iOS and Android. More than 7.5 million places with stories, in English, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese and Chinese. About StoryMapp →

  • Open data

    Long-time contributors to OpenStreetMap and open map data. We build on it and give back to it.

    Attribution →

  • Operations

    We run what we build: deployment, monitoring and DevOps for pipelines that have to work unattended.

  • Privacy by design

    GDPR from the first commit. Developed and operated in Germany; location never leaves the phone.

    Accurate place-based stories inside your own product, for tourism, travel and location platforms.
